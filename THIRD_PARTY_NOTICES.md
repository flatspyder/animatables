Material Symbols
================

The generated Kotlin vectors in `animatables/.../MaterialSymbols.kt` and
`catalog/src/shared/.../MaterialSymbols.kt` are derived from Google's
[Material Symbols](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons), licensed under
[Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/LICENSE).
Copyright Google LLC. The repository's [LICENSE](LICENSE) contains the license text.

`scripts/generate-material-symbols.py` records the pinned source revision and generates
only the Rounded, weight 400, unfilled, optical size 24 symbols used by this project.
The web gallery loads the matching symbol font through Google Fonts in its HTML header.
